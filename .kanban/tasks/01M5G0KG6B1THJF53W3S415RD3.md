## Change
- When a connection closes, its sessions have no owner, and they continue to run. Any connection that can see a session can take it (^ACPA-5c).
- This replaces the teardown in `bind` today, which calls `closeOpenSessions`.
- Decide what `waitForConnectionTeardown` means now. For example: it waits until the connection row is gone, and not until its sessions close. Write the decision in its doc comment.
- Never reintroduce "turns". The headless exit comes from the no-work state of the session (the work registry, idle `_meta.noWork`). Check that `acp-agent` headless (the bench harness) still exits when the work ends. A closed stdio connection still ends the process, if that is the behavior today. Keep it, and test it.

## Done when
- A failing test first (TDD): connection A makes a session and starts work, then A closes. The session continues, and has no owner. A new userDevice connection B takes it and gets its updates.
- The full `swift test` passes, and the bench tests (`python3 -m unittest discover -s bench`) pass. #acpa-5
