Found in mail step 4, T2, at the move to Router 6cb38629 and Extras d0d4653. Fixed in commit 1463f5e.

Problem: the test needs the quick agent run to end inside the settle period of the `start agent` call. The settle period waited on the real clock. Under the load of the full suite, the period ended first, and the call gave the pending envelope (1 failure in 1 run).

Fix: the test resolves its profile with a ManualRunClock through Router.useRunClock(_:) (new test helper Support/ManualClockProfile.swift). The test never advances the clock, so the period cannot end before the run.

Follow-up in T6: the other settle and timeout tests of AgentsToolTests use the same helper. #mail-step-4 #tests
