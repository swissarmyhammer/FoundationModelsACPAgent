## Dependency

^jex3g34 (the TranscriptSearchIndex). Do it after the update task of the index, because the stream reports its changes.

## Change

1. The index gives the host an `AsyncStream` of its changes:
   - the turns of a session that it indexed: the session id, the turn index, the date, and the text of the chunks of the turn by kind;
   - the sessions that it removed.
2. `turns(of:)`: the same turns of one session, from its items.
3. `allTurns()`: all turns, for a new Spotlight index.
4. This package does not import CoreSpotlight. The app writes the items and applies the rule of Q71 (no tool output and no thoughts). Give each kind, so the app can apply the rule.

## Tests

- A turn of a session gives one change with its turns; a delete gives one removal.
- `turns(of:)` and `allTurns()` give the same turns as the stream.
- A second index instance (the app's `client/` folder) has its own stream. #d207 #search
